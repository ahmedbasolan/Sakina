Sakina — square widget ideas

iPhone 18 home screen, widgets at their real size (about 162 pt). Four directions, A–D, side by side so you can compare how each sits among apps. Below the phone they're shown 1.5× bigger.

The four ideas
A · Next prayer — one thing: the coming prayer, its time, and a gold line that fills from the last prayer to this one. The lantern stays lit.
B · Verse — the mood verse you last chose, as on the prayer card. Short verses show Arabic and English; long ones show English only; the longest show a line inviting you to open it. Never cut off.
C · Prayer board — all five times for today, like a masjid board. The next prayer is gold with its filling line; passed ones fade. Hijri date on top.
D · Light the lantern — the same moment as the prayer card: say the phrase, tap the lantern, it lights. Tap it here to try.

No countdown on any of them, as on the prayer card. Tapping anywhere except D's lantern opens Sakina.
