Sakina — the island and the widget together

Island: what's happening now, at prayer times. Widget: the path you're on, through mood, Journey and reflection. They link up but never repeat each other. Hold the island, or switch to the Lock Screen, from the panel.

"Now"

  • The lantern and its phrase
  • The verse you chose
Never: Journeys, reflection, check-in

Square widget

"Your path"
  • How is your heart?
  • Your Journey's day
  • The question to reflect on
Never: prayer times, the lantern. The verse only when the prayer card is off.

How they link: you check in on the widget, the island carries the verse you got, and the widget then gives you the question about that same verse.
