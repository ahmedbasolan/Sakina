## Full description  (limit 4000)
Sakina is your daily Islamic companion. A quiet space to return to when life feels heavy, scattered, or overwhelming.

Tell the app how you're feeling. Sakina listens, then surfaces a verse from the Quran, its deeper context, and a gentle spiritual practice tailored to that exact moment. Not a generic reminder. Something that meets you where you are.

——

WHAT'S INSIDE

· Mood-Based Guidance — Choose how you're feeling and receive a relevant Quranic verse with scholarly tafsir and a practical spiritual action to take right now.

· Spiritual Journeys — Multi-day guided paths on tawakkul, rizq, salah, study, and more. One verse, one lesson, one practice per day.

· Prayer Times — Accurate times based on your location, with Hanafi Asr support and customisable daily reminders.

· Quran Library — Read complete surahs with Arabic, transliteration, and Sahih International translation.

· Du'a Collection — Supplications from the Quran and authentic Sunnah, organised by purpose and moment.

· Reflection Journal — Record your thoughts after each guidance session. Your private space, fully yours.

· Mood History — A calendar view of your spiritual check-ins over time, so you can see your journey at a glance.

· Lock Screen Verses — A complete ayah waiting for you at Tahajjud, morning, and evening remembrance. (Pro)

——

BUILT WITH CARE

Every verse is complete. Never truncated, never paraphrased. Every hadith is verified against its source. Quranic text follows the Uthmani script from Tanzil.net. Translations are Sahih International.

This is not an AI chatbot. It is a carefully curated library of classical Islamic scholarship, made accessible for the moments that matter most.
